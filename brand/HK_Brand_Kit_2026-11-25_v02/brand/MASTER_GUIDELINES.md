# Harrison Keith — Master Brand Guidelines (v02)

> **Authority Statement:** This document is the sole brand authority for all visual, digital, and frontend executions for Harrison Keith.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Creative Direction
- **Identity:** Personal identity for Harrison Keith (not a commercial business, apparel brand, or athletic company).
- **Core Narrative:** Technical Performance meets Modern Minimalism.
- **Tone:** Technical, Modern, Premium, Confident, Purposeful, Understated, Structured, Refined, Distinctive, Timeless, Professional.
- **Creative References:** Salomon, Arc'teryx, YETI, Specialized S-Works, Oakley, Bandit Running, Patagonia, Mountain Hardwear (aesthetic precision and restraint; do not copy their literal assets).

---

## 2. Core Identifiers & Prohibitions
- **Primary Brand Identifier:** `HARRISON KEITH`
- **Secondary Identifier:** `HK`
- **Strict Prohibition on Slogans:** Never invent company names, slogans, positioning statements, or taglines (e.g., NO "A Strategic Operator", "Built Through Exploration", "Strategy. Leadership. Exploration.").
- **Strict Prohibition on Coordinates:** Never use latitude or longitude coordinates anywhere.
- **Strict Prohibition on Military/Tactical Overlays:** Never use camouflage, crosshairs, dogtags, or tactical symbols.
- **Strict Prohibition on Topographic Clutter:** Never use dense contour maps, elevation numbers, or compass roses.

---

## 3. Strict 6-Color System
No other colors are approved.

| Category | Name | HEX Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neutral 01** | **Carbon** | `#141414` | Primary typography, dark surfaces, heavy marks |
| **Neutral 02** | **Concrete** | `#A6A6A6` | Secondary labels, hairlines, structural dividers |
| **Neutral 03** | **Chalk** | `#F4F4F4` | Canvas background, negative space, reversed typography |
| **Stripe 01** | **Signal Yellow** | `#E4BD25` | Signature Stripe 1, micro-indicators |
| **Stripe 02** | **Racing Red** | `#B74438` | Signature Stripe 2, primary focal accents |
| **Stripe 03** | **Alpine Blue** | `#438CA9` | Signature Stripe 3, cool technical balance |

---

## 4. Signature Color Stripes
- **Sequence:** Signal Yellow &rarr; Racing Red &rarr; Alpine Blue (Strict Order).
- **Geometry:** Forward-slanted at $12^\circ$ rake.
- **Proportion:** 3:1 height-to-width ratio, 25% inter-stripe gap.
- **Usage:** Supporting brand signature. Never used as oversized banners or background wallpaper.

---

## 5. Logo System Standards
1. **Primary HK Monogram:** Forward-slanted ($12^\circ$), bold construction with shared center spine for maximum legibility from 16px to massive display.
2. **Horizontal Lockup:** Monogram + vertical hairline + `HARRISON KEITH` + 3 Signature Stripes beneath wordmark.
3. **Centered Stacked Lockup:** Monogram centered on top, single-line extended `HARRISON KEITH` beneath, 3 Signature Stripes centered at bottom.
4. **Stacked Wordmark:** Standalone two-line typographic name mark (`HARRISON` over `KEITH`).
5. **Favicon:** Carbon square tile with Chalk monogram and micro Signal Yellow accent dot.
