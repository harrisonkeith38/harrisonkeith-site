# HK Brand Typography Specification (v02)

---

## 1. Primary Display Typeface — GT America Extended
- **Application:** Major headlines, oversized typography, prominent page titles, and wordmarks.
- **Characteristics:** Extended horizontal letterforms, bold geometric structure, confident weight.
- **Tracking:** 
  - Wordmark: `0.22em` - `0.28em`
  - Headlines: `-0.02em` - `0.02em`
- **Fallback Stack:** `GT America Extended, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`

---

## 2. Secondary Functional Typeface — FF DIN
- **Application:** Body copy, main navigation, subheadings, long descriptions, and interface components.
- **Characteristics:** Legible, engineered, structural, clean vertical geometry.
- **Tracking:** `0em` - `0.02em`
- **Fallback Stack:** `FF DIN, "DIN Alternate", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Inter, sans-serif`

---

## 3. Accent Technical Typeface — Eurostile Next
- **Application:** Micro labels, numerical info, technical annotations, category tags, and section indices.
- **Characteristics:** Squarish geometric curves, technical precision, wide tracking.
- **Tracking:** `0.12em` - `0.20em`
- **Fallback Stack:** `Eurostile Next, Eurostile, Microgramma, "JetBrains Mono", monospace, sans-serif`
