# HK Agent Rules & Guardrails

> Directives for all autonomous agents, LLM contributors, and frontend developers.

## Mandatory Implementation Directives
1. **The Sole Authority:** Always defer to `brand/MASTER_GUIDELINES.md`, `brand/ASSET_REGISTER.md`, and `brand/TYPOGRAPHY.md`.
2. **Strict Color Adherence:** Use only Carbon (`#141414`), Concrete (`#A6A6A6`), Chalk (`#F4F4F4`), Signal Yellow (`#E4BD25`), Racing Red (`#B74438`), and Alpine Blue (`#438CA9`).
3. **No Slogans or Coordinates:**
   - DO NOT invent taglines or brand slogans.
   - DO NOT insert geographic coordinates.
   - DO NOT introduce tactical, athletic, or outdoor merchandise clichés.
4. **Token Usage:** Always consume CSS variables from `tokens/css/hk-tokens.css` or Tailwind configuration mapped to those tokens.
5. **Asset Authority:** Load SVGs and visual assets exclusively from `assets/` (or mapped `public/brand/`).
6. **PROVISIONAL Assets:** Preserve all `PROVISIONAL` labels until approved master vector files explicitly replace them.
