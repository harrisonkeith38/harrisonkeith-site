## Brand Standards Summary

1. **Colors (Strict 6-Color System):**
   - **Carbon:** `#141414` (Deep near-black)
   - **Concrete:** `#A6A6A6` (Medium neutral gray)
   - **Chalk:** `#F4F4F4` (Clean near-white)
   - **Signal Yellow:** `#E4BD25` (Stripe 1)
   - **Racing Red:** `#B74438` (Stripe 2)
   - **Alpine Blue:** `#438CA9` (Stripe 3)

2. **Signature Stripes:**
   - Always in strict order: Yellow &rarr; Red &rarr; Blue.
   - Forward slant: $12^\circ$ rake.
   - Aspect ratio: 3:1 with 25% inter-stripe gap.

3. **Topography:**
   - Large-scale organic contour lines, thin $0.85\text{px}$ strokes in Concrete (`#A6A6A6`).
   - Broad spacing between contours, generous negative space.
   - Zero coordinates, elevation markers, compasses, or map legends.
